Failure-case gallery: source audio and licences

The "noisy" clips are mixtures built from the sources below. The "enhanced" clips are those mixtures processed by the model, which makes them derivative works under the same licences.

Speech
- VoiceBank+DEMAND test set: C. Valentini-Botinhao, "Noisy speech database for training speech enhancement algorithms and TTS models", University of Edinburgh, 2017. https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2117. CC BY 4.0.
- LibriSpeech test-clean (interfering talkers): V. Panayotov et al., 2015. https://www.openslr.org/12. CC BY 4.0.

Music (from the MUSAN corpus, D. Snyder, G. Chen, D. Povey, 2015, https://www.openslr.org/17)
- music-fma-0063: "Riding Music" by J Mano, from Life Rhymes / Showtime EP. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://freemusicarchive.org/music/J_Mano/Life_Rhymes_Showtime_EP
- music-jamendo-0005: "Minor Major" by AFTER WORK. CC BY-SA 3.0.
  Clips derived from these tracks (music_vocals_*, music_instrumental_*, music_speech_bg_*) are excerpts or remixes and are shared under the same CC BY-SA licence.

Reverberation uses simulated room impulse responses (pyroomacoustics), so it involves no third-party audio.
